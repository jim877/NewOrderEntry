## Severity
**Groups:** Fire, Water, Mold, Dust, Protein, Oil

**Levels:** 1, 2, 3, 5

**Detailed Severity Contaminants:**
- **Fire:** Heat, Soot, Odor, Extinguisher Powder, Remediation Debris
- **Water:** Water, Humidity, Musty Smell, Visible Mildew, Visible Mold, Sprinkler Chemical, Flood Cut Debris
- **Puffback:** Oil, Soot, Odor, Oily Film
